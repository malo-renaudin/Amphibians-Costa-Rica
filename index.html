Held-out evaluation

Fixed chronological protocol: train ≤2017, choose spatial mixing parameter using 2018–2021, test once on 2022–2024. Metrics compare parcel-level predictions with the fraction of test records belonging to species absent from the training parcel. Each eligible parcel counts equally (≥10 training and ≥5 test records).

Calculating…

Spearman ρ measures ranking; R² measures numerical accuracy (negative means worse than predicting the mean). Random 80/20 holdout of pre-2022 records is a secondary diagnostic, not the primary test.

What uncertainty means

The uncertainty map displays the width of a simple approximate 95% interval around each parcel's predicted probability. These intervals are not empirically calibrated. In the offline chronological test, their approximate parameter-interval coverage was only 24.5% across all records and 65.1% for human observations (against a nominal 95%). Even intervals widened for held-out sampling noise covered only 61.7% and 91.3%, respectively. They should be treated as relative uncertainty indicators, not reliable confidence limits. They ignore spatial dependence, observer selection and temporal shifts; they should not be interpreted as guaranteed 95% coverage.

GBIF records are opportunistic and not standardized surveys. Species new to a parcel are not necessarily species new to science. Rankings may reflect sampling patterns rather than conservation value. A parcel with no records is unknown, not low priority.
