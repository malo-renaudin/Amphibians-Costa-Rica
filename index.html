Method. The third map uses a smoothed Good–Turing estimator: (singletons + 20 × global singleton rate) / (records + 20). A singleton is a species observed exactly once within a parcel. The prior rate is 0.05397. Estimates are exploratory and assume additional records are drawn comparably to existing ones; survey design, accessibility, duplicate sampling and habitat can violate that assumption. Only parcels with at least one record are shown. Grid cells are approximately 0.09° latitude × 0.092° longitude; blank cells are not necessarily low-biodiversity.
