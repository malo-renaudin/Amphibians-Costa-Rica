Methods · Recurrent ridge discovery model

Question. Which ~10 km Costa Rican parcels are most likely to yield an observation of a species absent from that parcel's existing recorded inventory? A species new to a parcel is not necessarily new to science. Scores rank observed parcels; unsampled parcels cannot be reliably ranked.

Data. Costa Rican amphibian GBIF occurrence compilation (TSV), deduplicated by GBIF ID, with valid species names, coordinates within 8–11.3° N and 86–82.4° W, and dated records through 2024. Geographic cells use floor((latitude − 8)/0.09), floor((longitude + 86)/0.092). Human observations are analyzed separately from pooled record types.

Training example. A parcel at a historical cutoff year. The target is the fraction of records in the following 3–4 years belonging to species absent from that parcel's inventory at the cutoff. Repeated future records of the same previously unseen species count separately. This is a per-record discovery rate, not the expected number of distinct species per field visit.

Features. Current log(1 + number of records), recorded species richness, singleton-species fraction, and the proportion of records in the eight neighboring cells belonging to species unseen in the target cell. Each year's recurrent inputs are log(1 + annual record count), first appearances relative to earlier years within the 10-year input window, and annual distinct species count.

Model. h[t] = γ h[t−1] + x[t], starting from zero over the last ten years; p = clip(β₀ + β · [static features, h], 0, 1). Coefficients are learned by standardized ridge regression. γ ∈ {0, .25, .5, .75, .9, .99} and ridge α ∈ {.01, .1, 1, 10, 100} are chosen by lowest 2018–2021 validation MSE, not by test performance.

Chronological validation. Historical cutoff snapshots 2005, 2008, 2011, 2014 provide training targets in the following years. Cutoff 2017 / outcomes 2018–2021 select hyperparameters. The model is refitted on historical + validation snapshots, then evaluated once on cutoff 2021 / records 2022–2024. Eligible evaluation parcels require ≥10 historical and ≥5 future records. All eligible parcels contribute equally to MSE, R² and Spearman.

Deployment. The map uses coefficients refitted through 2021, applied to observations through 2024. It does not use post-2024 data. Thus the displayed probabilities are projections from a model validated for the 2022–2024 period, not independently validated predictions for 2025 onward. A full retraining on all 2024 data is a future improvement.

Limits. GBIF records reflect uneven observer effort and are not standardized surveys. A strong ranking on temporally held-out records does not establish causal conservation benefit, field-survey success probability, or spatial generalization. The uncertainty layer is a heuristic based on record counts, not a calibrated interval for this ridge model; use it only as an indicative warning. Absolute probability calibration and geographically held-out validation remain outstanding.

Reproducibility: GitHub repository. The coefficients below are embedded in the map source. Model benchmarks were run offline on the original records.
